Help & safety

Learn how the platform works, what each claim status means, and how to hand over an item safely.

How it works

1

Report

Describe the item you lost, or an item you found. Add the category, color, area and date. You get a unique Report ID like LF-1001 instantly.

2

Match

The system compares every lost report with every found report. When details line up, it suggests a possible match on both reports.

3

Verify

The claimant answers private questions set by the finder — scratches, contents, or case design. Only the real owner knows these details.

4

Recover

Once verified, meet at a safe public place. Hand over the item, then both sides confirm the return so the report closes cleanly.

Claim status explained

⏳

Pending

The claim was submitted and is waiting for the finder or admin to start review.

🔎

Under Verification

Answers are being checked against the private hints on the found report.

✅

Verified

Ownership is confirmed. The two parties can now arrange a safe handover.

📦

Returned

The item was handed over and both sides marked the return complete.

Safety tips for handover

📍

Meet in public places

Choose busy, well-lit locations such as malls, campuses or police collection points during daytime.

🪪

Verify before sharing details

Confirm ownership through the private verification step before you share any personal information.

💬

Use platform messaging first

Keep early conversations inside the app. Your phone number and address stay hidden until you decide to share.

👫

Bring a friend

For valuable items, bring someone with you and tell a family member where and when you are meeting.

✅

Check before confirming

Inspect the item carefully before you confirm the return on the platform.

🚩

Report suspicious behaviour

If something feels wrong, stop, leave the meeting, and report the account to admins.

Frequently asked questions

Is my phone number public?

No. Contact starts inside platform messaging. Your phone number and address are never shown publicly, and you share them only when you choose to.

How does verification work?

The finder adds private questions that only the real owner can answer. The claimant answers them, and the finder or an admin checks the answers before ownership is confirmed.

What if a claimant fails verification?

The claim is rejected and the report stays open for other claimants. Disputed cases go to admin review for a manual decision.

Can I delete my report?

Yes. Open My Reports and remove the report you no longer need. Removing a report also removes any claims linked to it.

Is there a fee?

No. Reporting, searching and claiming are completely free. The platform never asks for payment to return an item.

What if the item is valuable?

For high-value items, admins can assist the verification and ask for extra proof such as a receipt, serial number or original box before the handover.

Emergency. If you are in immediate danger or a crime is in progress, contact your local authorities or emergency services first. This platform is for lost and found items only and cannot replace the police.
