Help & Safety

Everything you need to know about using Lost & Found Citywide safely

📋 How to Report

Fill in as much detail as possible when reporting. Include photos, color, brand, and the exact location. More detail = higher chance of a match.

🔐 Verification System

Private details you provide are never shown publicly. Only a person answering verification questions correctly can claim an item. This protects you from false claims.

🤝 Safe Handover Tips

Always meet in a public, well-lit location such as a shopping mall or police station. Do not share your home address. Bring a friend if possible.

🛡️ Your Privacy

Your phone number and email are never shown to other users until you choose to share them after verification. All communication goes through our platform.

📞 Contact Admin

If you suspect fraud or a false claim, contact the admin team immediately. We review all disputes and can suspend suspicious accounts.

⏱️ How Long Are Reports Active?

Lost item reports remain active for 90 days and can be extended. Found items are active for 60 days. Returned items are archived in your history.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this service free to use? +
Yes, Lost & Found Citywide is completely free for all users. There are no charges for reporting, searching, or claiming items.
What if someone falsely claims my item? +
The verification system prevents this. Private details only the true owner knows are required to pass verification. Failed claims are logged and reviewed by admins.
How does the matching system work? +
Our system compares item category, description, color, brand, location, and date across lost and found reports to calculate a match confidence score. High-confidence matches trigger automatic notifications.
Can I report an item on behalf of someone else? +
Yes, you can report a found item you discovered, even if it's not yours. Just log in with your account and submit a found item report. The owner will be notified when matched.
What happens to unclaimed items? +
If a found item is not claimed within 60 days, the finder is notified and may donate it to a local charity or hand it to the nearest police station. The report is then archived.
